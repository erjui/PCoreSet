The research

Abstract

Active learning spends an annotation budget on selected examples. Knowledge distillation adds supervision from a teacher, including for unlabeled images. ActiveKD brings these together using a vision-language model that can serve as a zero-shot or few-shot teacher.

PCoreSet is the selection rule within this framework. It uses coverage in the student’s prediction-probability space to choose the next examples to label. The motivation is that distillation transfers useful structure from the teacher into this space. Experiments across 11 datasets evaluate selection strategies, annotation rounds, and both teacher settings.

Method

Train with ActiveKD

02

Cover probability space

Apply coreset selection to the student’s output probabilities to obtain a diverse annotation batch.

03

Label and repeat

Add the selected labels to the training set and continue the next active learning round.
